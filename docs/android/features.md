# MedRussia Android — Screen & Feature Breakdown

## 1. Onboarding & Authentication
* **Cinematic Onboarding**: Engaging visual introduction providing crucial clarity on NMC FMGL 2021 statutory norms, Russian climate and cultural orientation, and Indian hostel accommodations.
* **Authentication**: Streamlined login and registration interface featuring real-time client validation, error messages parsed via RFC 7807 problem details, and deep link email confirmation handling.

---

## 2. Main Dashboard & Navigation
* **Hero Admission Banner**:
  - For new candidates: High-impact banner prompting initial admission application submission.
  - For active applicants: Live status badge displaying current milestone (e.g., "MVD Ministry Invitation Issued").
* **4-Tile Quick Action Grid**: Fast navigation to 6-Year Calculator, NMC Eligibility Checker, Document Vault, and AI MD Counselor.
* **Featured University Carousel**: Horizontal scrolling cards highlighting top Russian medical universities with key metrics (tuition, rank, Indian dining availability).

---

## 3. University Catalog & Comparison
* **University List Screen**: Searchable catalog of 40+ government medical universities with live filtering by budget, recognition, and location.
* **Detailed University Profiles**: In-depth review of faculties, 6-year fee tables, campus photos, hostel facilities, and student reviews.
* **Side-by-Side Comparison**: Direct comparison screen evaluating tuition, living expenses, and clinical rotation facilities between chosen universities.

---

## 4. Financial & Compliance Calculators
* **6-Year Cost Planner**: Dynamic calculator allowing students to budget tuition, hostel, mess, insurance, and flights in INR, Russian Rubles (RUB), and USD.
* **NMC FMGL 2021 Evaluator**: Automated compliance checker verifying NEET qualification status, 12th PCB percentage, and age criteria.

---

## 5. 5-Stage Live Admission Tracker
* Visual step-by-step progress tracking across the 5 official milestones:
  1. *Application Submitted & Under Review*
  2. *Official University Admission Letter Issued*
  3. *MVD Ministry Electronic Visa Invitation Letter Issued*
  4. *Embassy Visa Stamping & Travel Coordination*
  5. *Arrival in Russia & University Matriculation*
* Displays administrative remarks, issued document references, and direct contact options for assigned counselors.

---

## 6. Student Document Vault
* Secure upload interface for student marksheets, NEET scorecards, and passport scans.
* Private document viewing rendered safely via ephemeral, server-generated signed URLs.
